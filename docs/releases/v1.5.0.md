# v1.5.0 — 麻将、2048、数独 三款新游戏，大厅扩至 32 款

## 中文

### 新增游戏

- **麻将**：大厅单入口，房间内可选「四川血战到底」或「广东鸡平胡」两种打法（2-4 人，带 AI）。
- **2048 竞速**：同一起点各滑各的 4x4，先合成 2048 者胜；无路可走则按最高分判胜负，可 1 人练习。
- **数独竞速**：同一道唯一解题，独立填格，先填完者胜；填错扣命、可用提示、有计时，1-4 人。

### 麻将玩法

- **血战到底**：一家胡后继续打，直到三家胡或牌墙摸空；关闭则首家胡即结束本局。
- **换三张**：开局先各选 3 张同花色牌与对家交换，再选定缺花色。
- **刮风下雨（杠分）**：杠牌即时结算，分数计入本局结果。
- **一炮多响**：同一张弃牌可被多家同时胡，全部响应完再推进。
- **查大叫 / 查花猪**：流局时未听牌的玩家，或仍持缺门花色的「花猪」，向其余玩家赔付。
- **定缺与只碰不吃**：须先打完缺门花色才能胡；四川不可吃牌。
- **买码**：广东打法胡牌后从牌尾买最多 4 张，每张字牌 +1 番，有翻牌动画。
- **封顶 / 起胡番数**：广东可设最大番（无/3/4/5）与最小起胡番（0/1/3）。
- **多局制与坐庄轮换**：局末结算累计积分，下一局由胡牌者坐庄，连打不中断。
- **红中百搭**：广东可选规则，红中（中）当万能牌，可替代任意牌凑面子/对子；游戏中角落常显「第 N 局 · 红中百搭」，其他玩家大厅也可看到。
- **特殊番型**：十三幺、大三元、大四喜，无需满足标准 4 面子 + 1 对结构即可胡牌。
- **规则细节**：字牌/风牌可碰；吃/碰后的弃牌正确移出牌河；任何胡牌至少平胡 1 番。

### 优化

- **麻将规则面板**：房内选四川/广东模式并勾选规则，其他玩家只读查看。
- **结算界面**：新增「下一局」主按钮，显示当前局数，可连续开打；牌摸完时明确显示「流局·平局 / 你胡了！/ 本局未胡」。
- **新摸牌高亮 / 定缺指示**：刚摸的牌青色发光标「新」；手牌顶部常显缺门花色；首局开局即选缺门花色（手机端同样正常）。
- **续局体验**：非房主可点「再来一局」请求续局（房主批准），其他人浮层变为「继续游戏」。
- **部分游戏可单人开局**（2048/数独/扫雷/炸弹/24点/西瓜/你画我猜），等待席精简，房主可移除 AI。
- **UNO +4 挑战**：被 +4 者可接受或质疑，结果 toast 揭示；手牌多行居中、对手牌背限高。
- **达芬奇 / 骗子酒馆 / 西瓜大战**：对手牌公开花色并有「猜鬼牌」按钮；出牌可多选；双西瓜相撞双双消除并计分。
- **移动端与封面**：头像选择器改居中卡片、棋盘无滚动死角；封面转 WebP，加载更快；配合 gzip 压缩与局域网 IP 二维码，手机扫码即玩。
- **安装包瘦身**：Android 安装包从约 123 MB 降到约 30 MB —— 封面图 PNG 转 WebP（省下最大的一块）、按 CPU 架构拆包、只打生产依赖。
- **开发者调试接口**：新增面向开发者的调试/日志 HTTP API，开发时可查看当前活跃房间；默认关闭，需环境变量 `GAMENEST_DEBUG=1` 开启，关闭时接口返回 404，正常局域网对局不会暴露房间内部数据。
- **房间踢人**：等待房间房主可移出真人玩家（未开局时），被踢者收到提示并返回大厅；「移除 AI」与「踢人」按钮统一样式（红色 ✕）。
- **移动端适配**：手机小屏把对手牌背/明牌/弃牌缩小、中央弃牌区上移约 15%（大屏/平板不变），棋盘更紧凑、不撑出滚动。

### 修复

- **等待房间**：修复「移除 AI / 踢人」按钮点了没反应（此前事件未绑定）；移除 AI 后不再把剩余电脑重排到真人座位（此前会导致"电脑消失/错位"）；换座不再丢失电脑（两个电脑相邻对换时曾消失一个）。
- **国际象棋**：状态栏不再被截断。
- **规则对齐**：大老二首出必须带 ♦3；五子棋长连不算胜（须恰 5 连）；爆炸猫改官方牌组（每人 4 张、新增 Nope 卡）；抽鬼牌强制从左邻抽；拉密破冰鬼牌计 30 分；德州扑克修正加注结算。

## English

### New games

- **Mahjong**: a single lobby entry with two in-room modes — Sichuan blood battle (四川血战到底) and Cantonese chicken-hand (广东鸡平胡); 2-4 players with AI.
- **2048 racing**: everyone races their own 4x4 from the same seed; the first to reach 2048 wins, or the highest score if all lock up; solo friendly.
- **Sudoku racing**: everyone races the same unique-solution puzzle; the first to fill all cells wins; wrong fills cost a life, hints and a timer included; 1-4 players.

### Mahjong rules

- **Blood battle (血战到底)**: play continues after a win until 3 players win or the wall runs out; off = round ends at the first win.
- **Swap 3 (换三张)**: at the start each player swaps 3 same-suit tiles with the player across, then picks a void (定缺) suit.
- **Gang scoring (刮风下雨)**: kongs settle instantly and roll into the round total.
- **Multi-win (一炮多响)**: one discard can be won by several players at once; play proceeds once all respond.
- **Big-call / Flower-pig (查大叫 / 查花猪)**: on a draw, players not listening, and players still holding a void suit ("flower pig"), pay the others.
- **Void suit & no-chow (定缺)**: you must clear the void suit before winning; Sichuan plays no-chow.
- **Buy tiles (买码)**: after a win, draw up to 4 from the wall tail; each honour adds a fan (flip animation).
- **Max / min fan caps**: Cantonese lets you set the max-fan cap (none/3/4/5) and the minimum fan to win (0/1/3).
- **Multi-round + dealer rotation**: each round settles a cumulative score; the winner deals next, so play continues.
- **Red Dragon Wild (红中百搭)**: Cantonese opt-in — the Red Dragon (中) acts as a wild tile for any meld/pair; shown in-room and in-game for everyone.
- **Special hands**: Thirteen Orphans (十三幺), Big Three Dragons (大三元), Big Four Winds (大四喜) — win without the standard 4 melds + 1 pair.
- **Rule details**: honour/wind tiles can be ponged; claimed tiles leave the pile; any win scores at least plain-win 1 fan.

### Improvements

- **Rules panel**: choose the Sichuan/Cantonese mode and tick rules in-room; others view it read-only.
- **Round-end panel**: a "Next round" button plus the round number keeps play going; when the wall runs out the result clearly shows draw / you-win / not-a-win.
- **New-draw highlight & void indicator**: the tile you just drew glows cyan with a "新" mark; your hand shows the void suit; the first round opens with the void-suit pick (working on phones too).
- **Continue-the-game flow**: a non-host can request a new round (host approves); others see "Continue".
- **Solo-start games** (2048/sudoku/minesweeper/numberbomb/24-point/watermelon/drawguess) with a tighter waiting room and a host remove-bot button.
- **UNO +4 challenge**: the +4 target accepts or challenges and a toast reveals the truth; hands wrap into rows.
- **Davinci / Liars' bar / Watermelon**: opponents show colour with a "guess the joker" button; multi-select play; two max melons merge and score.
- **Mobile & covers**: centred avatar drawer, no dead scroll; WebP covers load the lobby faster with gzip and a LAN-IP QR.
- **Smaller install**: the Android package went from about 123 MB to about 30 MB — cover art converted from PNG to WebP (the single biggest saving), per-CPU-architecture builds, and production-only dependencies.
- **Developer debug API**: a debug/logging HTTP API for inspecting active rooms during development; off by default (enable with `GAMENEST_DEBUG=1`), and when off the endpoints return 404, so a normal LAN game never exposes room internals.
- **Host kick**: in the waiting room the host can remove a human player (pre-game); the kicked player is returned to the lobby. The "remove bot" and "kick" buttons share one red-✕ style.
- **Mobile layout**: on small phones the opponents' hands/melds/discards are drawn smaller and the central discard zone rises ~15% (unchanged on large screens/tablets), keeping the board compact without scrolling.

### Fixes

- **Waiting room**: the remove-AI / kick buttons now respond (previously unbound); removing a bot no longer re-packs the remaining bots onto human seats (which made "computers vanish / jumble"); seat swap no longer loses a bot when two adjacent bots swap.
- **Chess**: the status bar is no longer clipped.
- **Rules aligned**: Big Two's opening must include ♦3; Gomoku overlines no longer win (exactly 5); Exploding Kittens uses the official deck (4 per player + a Nope card); Old Maid draws from your left; Rummikub jokers score 30 breaking the ice; Texas fixed the betting order.
